# ECHU-S-SALES-REPORT-DASHBOARD-PROJECT

**Project Overview**



This project presents a complete Sales Performance Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides actionable insights into revenue, profit, customer behavior, product performance, and sales trends using a retail sales dataset.

The project focuses on transforming raw sales data into a clean, interactive, and visually engaging business intelligence dashboard for decision-making.

**Dashboard Insights**

1. Regional Profit Analysis

The North region generated the highest profit, followed closely by the East region.

Key Observation
North region performed best overall
South region generated the lowest profit
Regional sales performance is relatively balanced

2. Product Category Performance

Furniture and Clothing categories generated the highest profits.

Key Observation
Furniture leads in profitability
Electronics generated the lowest profit
Product diversification contributes positively to revenue

3. Sales Channel Analysis

Retail and Online sales channels contributed almost equally to total profit.

Key Observation
Retail slightly outperformed Online
Balanced channel performance reduces business risk

4. Customer Type Analysis

New and Returning customers contributed nearly equally to quantity sold.

**Key Observation**


Returning customers remain highly valuable.

Strong customer acquisition strategy exists.



**DAX Measures Used**

**Total Revenue**
Total Revenue = SUM('Sales Data'[Revenue])

**Total Cost**
Total Cost = SUM('Sales Data'[Cost ])

**Total Profit**
Total Profit = SUM('Sales Data'[Profit])

**Quantity Sold**
Quantity Sold = SUM('Sales Data'[Quantity Sold])

**Profit Margin**
Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue], 0)
